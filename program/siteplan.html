Site Plan - WDD 131

Site Name

Tierra & Trazo

"Tierra" is earth in Spanish and "Trazo" is like brushstroke. The name reflects the handmade art of the shop and is short and easy to remember.

Site Purpose

Is an online tool for a local art supply shop. It provides visitors with online help to see product catalog, information about the shop's location and schedule

Scenarios

  • I'm a beginner. What starter kits does the shop carry?
  • I need to contact the shop. How can I reach them?

Color Schema

  • #5c3d2e for the header, navigation background, and footer
  • #c4693a for buttons, links, and accent elements
  • #d4a843 for highlights and active states
  • #f5f0e8 for section backgrounds and cards

Typography

  • Playfair Display for headings and the site logo
  • Lato for body text, navigation, and labels
